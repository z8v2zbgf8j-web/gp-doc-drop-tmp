# 16 Personality Types — Real-Time Sales Pattern Recognition

## Purpose
Identify prospect personality type within the first 2-3 minutes of conversation.
Tailor pitch delivery, objection handling, and close style to match their thinking pattern.
Combined with NEPQ questioning framework for maximum trust and conversion.

---

## Quick-Read Signals (First 90 Seconds)

| Signal | Indicator | Likely Types |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Asks about ROI/numbers immediately | Analytical thinker | ISTJ, INTJ, ESTJ, ENTJ |
| Asks about other customers' experience | Social proof seeker | ESFJ, ENFJ, ISFJ, ESFP |
| Wants to understand the "why" | Vision/meaning driven | INFJ, INFP, ENFP, ENTP |
| Wants to see it working NOW | Action-oriented | ESTP, ISTP, ENTJ, ESTJ |
| Asks a lot of questions before committing | Process-oriented | ISTJ, ISFJ, INTP, INTJ |
| Gets excited quickly, talks fast | Enthusiasm-driven | ENFP, ESFP, ENTP, ESTP |

## The 16 Types — Pitch Adaptation

### ANALYSTS (NT)

**INTJ — The Architect**
- *Reads as:* Quiet, direct, skeptical, asks precise questions
- *Wants:* Long-term strategic value, not hype
- *Pitch style:* Data-heavy, show the 10-year emission schedule, explain tokenomics math
- *NEPQ approach:* Situation + Problem questions — let them discover the gap themselves
- *Close:* "Based on what you've seen, does the math work for your situation?"
- *Avoid:* Emotional appeals, urgency tactics, name-dropping

**INTP — The Logician**
- *Reads as:* Curious, asks "how does it work" repeatedly, wants to understand the system
- *Wants:* Technical depth, intellectual honesty
- *Pitch style:* Explain the architecture — smart contracts, ERCOT integration, API pipeline
- *NEPQ approach:* Problem + Implication questions — connect technical gaps to real losses
- *Close:* "Would you want to see the technical docs before deciding?"
- *Avoid:* Glossing over details, "just trust me" energy

**ENTJ — The Commander**
- *Reads as:* Decisive, time-conscious, "get to the point"
- *Wants:* Control, ROI, competitive advantage
- *Pitch style:* Lead with numbers — $67.50/event Powerwall discharge, 14.4:1 LTV:CAC
- *NEPQ approach:* Short situation, jump to Need-Payoff — show the money
- *Close:* "When do you want to start earning?"
- *Avoid:* Long explanations, tentative language, asking too many questions

**ENTP — The Debater**
- *Reads as:* Challenges everything, plays devil's advocate, enjoys the back-and-forth
- *Wants:* Innovation, to be first, intellectual sparring
- *Pitch style:* Frame as cutting-edge — "first energy platform tokenizing solar exports on Arbitrum"
- *NEPQ approach:* Let them challenge, use their objections as discovery questions back
- *Close:* "You clearly get this better than most — want to be in the first cohort?"
- *Avoid:* Getting defensive, scripted responses, being boring

### DIPLOMATS (NF)

**INFJ — The Advocate**
- *Reads as:* Quiet but deeply engaged, asks about impact and mission
- *Wants:* Purpose, alignment with values, making a difference
- *Pitch style:* Lead with mission — Texas energy independence, consumer protection, green energy
- *NEPQ approach:* Deep Problem + Implication — connect energy waste to environmental/community impact
- *Close:* "This seems aligned with what matters to you — is there anything holding you back?"
- *Avoid:* Hard sells, money-first framing, surface-level conversation

**INFP — The Mediator**
- *Reads as:* Idealistic, wants authenticity, sensitive to pressure
- *Wants:* Genuine connection, to feel good about the decision
- *Pitch style:* Story-driven — real customer scenarios, the founding story, the vision
- *NEPQ approach:* Gentle Problem questions, emphasis on emotional payoff
- *Close:* "Take your time — but imagine what it'd feel like to actually earn from your solar"
- *Avoid:* ANY pressure, countdown timers, "limited spots" urgency

**ENFJ — The Protagonist**
- *Reads as:* Warm, asks about the team, wants to help/contribute
- *Wants:* To be part of something, community, shared mission
- *Pitch style:* "Join the movement" framing — leaderboard, community, badges
- *NEPQ approach:* Need-Payoff heavy — paint the picture of them as an early adopter leading others
- *Close:* "You'd be exactly the kind of person who brings their neighbors into this"
- *Avoid:* Transactional tone, isolation framing

**ENFP — The Campaigner**
- *Reads as:* Enthusiastic, jumps between topics, big-picture thinker
- *Wants:* Excitement, possibility, freedom
- *Pitch style:* Vision-forward — "imagine your house paying you every month"
- *NEPQ approach:* Light Situation, heavy Need-Payoff — keep momentum, don't bog down
- *Close:* "Want to get started and see your first GPT tokens mint?"
- *Avoid:* Too many details early, dampening enthusiasm with caveats

### SENTINELS (SJ)

**ISTJ — The Logistician**
- *Reads as:* Methodical, wants documentation, references, proof
- *Wants:* Reliability, track record, clear process
- *Pitch style:* Step-by-step — "Here's exactly how it works: signup → meter connect → data flows → tokens mint"
- *NEPQ approach:* Thorough Situation questions, establish current pain with specifics
- *Close:* "Would you like to see the full onboarding checklist?"
- *Avoid:* Vague promises, skipping steps, "just trust the process"

**ISFJ — The Defender**
- *Reads as:* Polite, risk-averse, asks "what if" scenarios
- *Wants:* Safety, guarantees, social proof
- *Pitch style:* Testimonials, case studies, "82 families already enrolled"
- *NEPQ approach:* Problem questions focused on security concerns — what happens if rates spike?
- *Close:* "Would it help to see what other Houston homeowners are earning?"
- *Avoid:* Disruption framing, "revolutionary" language, pressure

**ESTJ — The Executive**
- *Reads as:* Organized, time-aware, wants credentials and authority
- *Wants:* Proven results, efficiency, clear hierarchy
- *Pitch style:* Business case — ROI table, payback period, tier comparison
- *NEPQ approach:* Efficient Situation → direct Problem → fast Need-Payoff
- *Close:* "Based on your usage, you'd be in the Earner tier. Want me to set that up?"
- *Avoid:* Casual tone, unstructured conversation, wasting their time

**ESFJ — The Consul**
- *Reads as:* Friendly, relationship-focused, asks about family/community impact
- *Wants:* Harmony, to do right by family, community approval
- *Pitch style:* Family savings angle — "your panels are already generating, let's make sure your family benefits"
- *NEPQ approach:* Situation focused on family energy costs, Problem around leaving money on the table
- *Close:* "Your family's already doing the hard part — this just makes it pay off"
- *Avoid:* Technical jargon, cold/transactional tone

### EXPLORERS (SP)

**ISTP — The Virtuoso**
- *Reads as:* Quiet, practical, wants to tinker and understand mechanics
- *Wants:* How it works technically, hands-on control
- *Pitch style:* Show the dashboard, the API, the real-time data flow
- *NEPQ approach:* Minimal questions — show, don't tell. Let them explore
- *Close:* "Want to connect your meter and see your data flowing in real-time?"
- *Avoid:* Over-explaining, emotional appeals, hand-holding

**ISFP — The Adventurer**
- *Reads as:* Gentle, present-focused, values personal expression
- *Wants:* Authenticity, personal meaning, low-pressure
- *Pitch style:* Personal story, the beauty of energy independence
- *NEPQ approach:* Light touch — Situation + Need-Payoff with emphasis on personal freedom
- *Close:* "This might not be for everyone, but it seems like it fits your setup perfectly"
- *Avoid:* Corporate energy, aggressive follow-up

**ESTP — The Entrepreneur**
- *Reads as:* Fast-talking, action-oriented, competitive, "what's the deal?"
- *Wants:* Quick wins, competitive edge, action NOW
- *Pitch style:* "Your neighbor's panels are earning and yours aren't" — competitive framing
- *NEPQ approach:* Skip to Problem + Need-Payoff — they don't need warm-up
- *Close:* "Let's get you signed up right now — takes 3 minutes"
- *Avoid:* Long explanations, hesitation, analysis paralysis

**ESFP — The Entertainer**
- *Reads as:* Fun, social, wants to see the cool factor
- *Wants:* Excitement, social bragging rights, instant gratification
- *Pitch style:* Leaderboard, badges, "show your friends your earnings"
- *NEPQ approach:* Light and fun — keep it conversational, not formal
- *Close:* "Imagine showing your friends your monthly GPT earnings — want to get set up?"
- *Avoid:* Dry presentations, heavy paperwork talk, boring them

## Real-Time Detection Protocol

During a live call, Star should:

1. **Listen for 90 seconds** — note speech pace, question style, emotional tone
2. **Classify into quadrant** — Analyst / Diplomat / Sentinel / Explorer
3. **Narrow to 2-3 types** — based on I/E and specific signals
4. **Adapt pitch in real-time** — shift language, examples, close style
5. **Log the type** — write to vault for future interactions with same prospect

## Integration with NEPQ

Jeremy Miner's NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning) maps directly:

| NEPQ Phase | Best For Types | Approach |
|------------|---------------|----------|
| Situation Questions | SJ types (ISTJ, ISFJ, ESTJ, ESFJ) | Thorough, build trust through process |
| Problem Questions | NT types (INTJ, INTP, ENTJ, ENTP) | Let them discover the gap logically |
| Implication Questions | NF types (INFJ, INFP, ENFJ, ENFP) | Connect to values and impact |
| Need-Payoff Questions | SP types (ISTP, ISFP, ESTP, ESFP) | Show the result, skip the theory |
